# 📸 Upload Your Kenyan Entrepreneur Photos

## How to Add Your Photos Like the Watu Example

### 1. **Photo Requirements**
- **Format**: JPG, PNG, or WebP
- **Size**: 1920x1080 pixels or similar high resolution
- **Style**: Clear, sharp Kenyan entrepreneurs in the foreground (like the Watu boda riders example)
- **Content**: Real Kenyan entrepreneurs in action

### 2. **Upload Your Photos**
1. **Place your photos** in the `public/images/` directory
2. **Name them exactly** as shown below:
   - `boda-rider-michael.jpg` - Your boda boda rider photo
   - `market-vendor-grace.jpg` - Your market vendor photo  
   - `digital-seller-sarah.jpg` - Your digital seller photo
   - `craft-vendor-amina.jpg` - Your craft vendor photo

### 3. **Perfect Photo Examples** (Based on Your Uploads)
- **Boda boda riders** with passengers (transportation entrepreneurs)
- **Market vendors** with fresh produce (food entrepreneurs)
- **Digital sellers** with smartphones/online business (tech entrepreneurs)
- **Craft vendors** with handmade goods (artisan entrepreneurs)

### 4. **Photo Style Guidelines**
- ✅ **Sharp, clear entrepreneurs** in the foreground
- ✅ **Blurred background** for depth of field
- ✅ **Natural lighting** and authentic settings
- ✅ **Kenyan people** in their work environment
- ✅ **Professional quality** but authentic feel

### 5. **Current Status**
The slideshow is ready and will automatically:
- ✅ Display your photos full-width
- ✅ Show entrepreneur info overlay (name, location, description)
- ✅ Auto-advance every 5 seconds
- ✅ Include navigation controls
- ✅ Work on all devices

### 6. **Next Steps**
1. Upload your 4 photos to `public/images/`
2. Name them exactly as specified above
3. Refresh your browser at http://localhost:8082/
4. Your slideshow will display like the Watu example!

The slideshow will showcase your authentic Kenyan entrepreneurs with clear, sharp images and professional styling! 🇰🇪✨
